# Keyword Research & Page Mapping

Method: keywords were selected for **commercial or transactional intent** (people looking to hire, buy or compare) and mapped to one page each to avoid cannibalisation. No search-volume or difficulty numbers are given — they weren't verifiable without a paid tool. Validate priorities with **Google Search Console** (free, after 4–8 weeks of data) and the free tier of Google Keyword Planner.

Intent key: **T** = transactional (ready to hire/buy), **C** = commercial investigation (comparing), **I** = informational (learning, earlier in the funnel).

### International modifiers (use naturally in copy, not as separate pages)
