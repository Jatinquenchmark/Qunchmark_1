Research date: 24 November 2026. Third-party listings change often and sometimes disagree — **re-verify any competitor fact before using it publicly**, and never publish claims about a competitor that you can't source.

### Where BrokrSuite can honestly differentiate
Based only on verified BrokrSuite capabilities:
1. **Built for brokers and property dealers, not just developers.** Sell.Do and LeadSquared lean toward builders' project inventory; BrokrSuite centres on a broker's resale/rental property inventory, leads, clients and follow-ups.
2. **Properties + clients + team in one mobile app.** Property inventory, public listings, leads, follow-ups and team assignment live together on iPhone and Android — ideal for brokers who work from site visits.
3. **Simplicity for small and mid-sized teams.** Positioned to replace "WhatsApp + Excel + notebook" without enterprise setup.
4. **Public property publishing** directly from the inventory (confirmed by QuenchMark).
5. **Pricing:** officially "being finalised" — once set, a transparent, broker-friendly price can become a differentiator. Do not advertise as "cheaper than X" until prices are published and comparable.

### Gaps to watch (features competitors advertise)
WhatsApp integration, calling/dialler, channel partner portals, automated lead capture from property portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing), and post-sales/booking workflows. If BrokrSuite adds any of these, update the product page and FAQs — don't claim them before they ship.

### Comparison content
A "Sell.Do alternative" page on brokrsuite.in can work, but only with: a dated, sourced feature table; fair language ("best for…"); no claims about competitor pricing beyond what they publish. Until verified data exists, prefer "How to choose a real estate CRM" content that explains criteria without naming competitors.

## 2. QuenchMark — technology development services

The market is crowded: thousands of Indian agencies and freelancers, global marketplaces (Upwork, Toptal) and directory-ranked firms on Clutch/GoodFirms. Typical competitor patterns:
- Generic "top-rated" claims, inflated project counts and stock testimonials.
- Service pages that list technologies but say little about outcomes or process.
- Weak proof for SaaS/product capability.

### QuenchMark's credible advantages
1. **Builds and operates its own live products** (BrokrSuite on both app stores, DMGennie, QuantMentor) — tangible proof most agencies can't show.
2. **End-to-end team**: design, web, mobile, backend, AI, cloud.
3. **Transparent process**: written scope and estimate before work; content on the site explains costs honestly.
4. **Real estate depth** through BrokrSuite — a natural niche for custom real estate software projects.

### Gaps to close
- Client case studies and testimonials (with permission).
- Directory profiles with genuine reviews (Clutch, GoodFirms, Google Business Profile).
- Founder-led thought leadership on LinkedIn.
- Clear team page (names/roles) once the team agrees to be shown.
