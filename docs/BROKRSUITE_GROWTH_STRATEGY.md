# BrokrSuite Growth Strategy

Goal: turn real estate businesses into **product demos** and demos into **paying customers**. Primary CTA everywhere: **Book a Demo**.

## 1. Positioning

- **Category:** All-in-one real estate CRM and business management platform.
- **Headline:** Your entire real estate business. One powerful platform.
- **Tagline:** Manage smarter. Sell better. Grow faster.
- **For:** brokers, property dealers, real estate agencies, consultants, sales teams and channel partners — especially teams of 1–50 still running on WhatsApp + Excel.
- **Promise:** properties, leads, clients, follow-ups, daily tasks and your team in one app on iPhone and Android, with public property listings.
- **Proof we can use today:** live on the App Store and Google Play; built and supported by QuenchMark Technologies. (Add customer stories once customers agree to be named.)

Verified capabilities only: property inventory & public listings · lead & client management · follow-up reminders & daily tasks · team management (add members, assign leads & properties, track activity) · business analytics dashboard · iOS & Android apps. Pricing: on request (being finalised).

## 2. Funnel

```
Awareness → Interest → Demo booked → Demo held → Trial/onboarding → Paid → Referral
```

| Stage | Channels | Conversion asset | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | LinkedIn, Instagram/Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, broker WhatsApp communities, local real estate associations, brokrsuite.in SEO | Short demos of one feature each | Reach, profile visits |
| Interest | quenchmark.org/brokrsuite, brokrsuite.in, app store pages | Feature pages, screenshots, FAQ | Page views, app store clicks |
| Demo booked | Demo form (`/brokrsuite#demo`), WhatsApp pre-filled message, direct outreach | "Book a Free Demo" | `generate_lead` (lead_type=brokrsuite) |
| Demo held | WhatsApp/phone/video call within 24 h | Demo script (below) | Show-up rate |
| Onboarding | Assisted setup: import properties, add team | Onboarding checklist | Activated accounts (≥10 properties, ≥1 teammate) |
| Paid | Proposal after onboarding value is visible | Pricing on request | Demo → paid % |
| Referral | Happy customers | Referral offer | Referred demos |

**Speed matters:** reply to every demo request on WhatsApp within 1 working hour where possible; book the demo in the same conversation.

## 3. Demo script (20–25 minutes)

1. **Discover (5 min)** — "How do you manage properties and leads today? How many people on your team? What falls through the cracks?" Write down their exact words.
2. **Show their day (10 min)** — use their scenario, not a feature tour:
   - Add a property they recently listed → publish it publicly.
   - Add a lead → set a follow-up reminder → show today's tasks.
   - Assign the lead and property to an agent → show team activity.
   - Open the dashboard → "this is what you'd see every morning".
   - Do it all on the phone app.
3. **Confirm value (3 min)** — "Which of these would save you the most time?"
4. **Handle questions (5 min)** — see objections below.
5. **Next step (2 min)** — "Shall we set up your account with your first 20 properties this week?" Agree a date.

## 4. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We manage fine on WhatsApp and Excel." | "Most of our users started there. The problem shows up when a lead is forgotten or an agent leaves with the contacts. Let's import 20 properties and see if it's easier — no commitment." |
| "My team won't use it." | "That's why it's a phone app built around their day — properties, leads and follow-ups. We'll help onboard the team and you'll see who's using it." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Pricing depends on team size; plans exist for individual brokers, growing teams and larger agencies. Let me understand your team and I'll share the right option." (Don't quote unpublished numbers.) |
| "We already use another CRM." | "What do you like and dislike about it? BrokrSuite is built specifically for brokers' property inventory plus leads — if that's a gap, it's worth comparing." (Never disparage competitors.) |
| "Is our data safe?" | Explain data handling honestly per the BrokrSuite privacy policy at brokrsuite.in; offer to answer technical questions from the QuenchMark team. |
| "We're too small." | "It works for a single broker on one phone — and grows when you add people." |

## 5. Demo-to-customer process

1. Within 24 h of the demo: WhatsApp recap with the 3 benefits they cared about + app download links.
2. Day 2–3: assisted setup call (import properties, add team).
3. Day 7: check-in — usage review, answer questions.
4. Day 10–14: pricing proposal based on team size; close.
5. Day 30: success check + referral ask.

Track every lead in a simple sheet or CRM: source (from the enquiry email's Lead source block), demo date, outcome, plan, reason lost.

## 6. Referral strategy
- Ask at the moment of value (after their first converted lead or first full month).
- Offer something proportional and honest, e.g. an extended period or onboarding help for both sides (decide the offer internally; publish terms clearly).
- Give customers a ready-to-forward WhatsApp message with a UTM-tagged link: `?utm_source=referral&utm_medium=whatsapp&utm_campaign=brokr-referral`.

## 7. Partnerships
- **Real estate associations / broker groups:** free workshop "Digitise your brokerage in 30 days" with a live BrokrSuite demo.
- **Property photographers, legal and loan advisors** who serve brokers: co-marketing and referral arrangements.
- **Training institutes for real estate agents:** include BrokrSuite in practical sessions.
- **Property portals:** explore listing integrations only after confirming technical feasibility.
