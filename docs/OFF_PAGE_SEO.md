## 2. Ethical link acquisition

1. **Guest articles** on startup, SaaS, real estate and small-business sites — offer genuinely useful pieces (e.g. "What real estate brokers should look for in a CRM", "How to scope an MVP"). Target publications that accept contributor posts and link to quenchmark.org/insights or relevant service pages naturally.
2. **Founder content:** LinkedIn articles, podcast guest spots (Indian startup and SaaS podcasts), local tech meetups and college talks.
3. **Technology partner listings:** if you build on Vercel, AWS, Razorpay, Shopify etc., check their partner/agency directories (most require proof of client projects).
4. **Real estate associations:** sponsor or speak at local broker association events — event pages often link to sponsors and speakers.
5. **Unlinked mentions:** search for "QuenchMark" / "BrokrSuite" mentions without links and politely ask for a link.
6. **Product Hunt / Indie Hackers / Hacker News Show HN:** for BrokrSuite and DMGennie — honest launch posts.
7. **Resource pages:** universities/incubators listing local tech service providers.
8. **Referral partners:** agencies, consultants and freelancers who send you overflow work — a partners section linking both ways is fine when the relationship is real.

## 3. Review strategy
After each successful project or BrokrSuite onboarding: ask for an honest review on Google Business Profile / Clutch / GoodFirms / app stores. Never offer payment, discounts or gifts in exchange for positive reviews, and never write reviews yourself.

## 4. Tracking
Keep a sheet: platform, URL, date submitted, status, profile link, reviews count. Check referral traffic in GA4 (Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, source contains the platform).
