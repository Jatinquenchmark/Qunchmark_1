# Outreach Templates

Replace everything in `[brackets]`. Always reference something specific and true about the recipient. Keep first messages short; never send the same message to a list. Add a simple opt-out line to emails.

## A. Development services

### Cold email — website improvement
**Subject:** Quick idea for [Company]'s website

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [company website] and noticed [specific, observable issue — e.g. "the contact form isn't visible on mobile" / "pages take a while to load on 4G"].

At QuenchMark Technologies we build websites designed to turn visitors into enquiries — fast, mobile-first and search-friendly. I'd be happy to send three specific suggestions for your site, free, no strings attached.

Would that be useful?

[Your name]
QuenchMark Technologies · www.quenchmark.org
*If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.*
