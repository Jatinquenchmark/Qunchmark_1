# Client Acquisition Strategy (Outbound + Inbound)

SEO compounds slowly. Direct, personalised outreach produces conversations now. Run both. Templates are in `OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md`.

**Rules:** personalise every message to a genuine need; small daily volumes; respect opt-outs immediately; follow anti-spam and privacy rules (India's DPDP Act, GDPR/UK GDPR for European contacts, CAN-SPAM for the US). Never buy email lists or send bulk unsolicited WhatsApp messages — WhatsApp bans numbers that do.

## 1. Ideal customer profiles — development services

| Segment | Signal to look for | Lead offer | Best channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Startup founders | Pre-seed/seed, no technical co-founder, hiring "first developer" | MVP scoping call | LinkedIn, founder communities |
| SMEs with outdated websites | Slow, non-mobile site, no HTTPS, old copyright year | Free 5-point website review | Email, LinkedIn |
| Businesses without websites | Google Maps listing with no website | Website + Google Business setup proposal | Phone/WhatsApp (only if they list it publicly for business), in-person |
| Companies needing apps | Hiring mobile devs, customer complaints about no app | App scoping call | LinkedIn |
| CRM/ERP buyers | Job posts mentioning Excel-heavy ops, multiple tools | Process audit | LinkedIn, email |
| AI automation prospects | Support-heavy, repetitive back-office work | One-workflow AI pilot | LinkedIn |
| Digital agencies | Agencies selling websites/apps without in-house dev | White-label partnership | LinkedIn, email |
| Companies needing developers | Open dev roles for 60+ days | Dedicated developer trial | LinkedIn |
| International SMEs (US/UK/UAE/AU/SG/DE/CA) | Growing companies, open dev roles, outsourcing interest | Time-zone-overlap dedicated team | LinkedIn, email |

## 2. Weekly operating rhythm (founder + 1 person)

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Mon | Build 25 prospects (5 segments × 5) in a sheet: name, company, signal, source |
| Tue–Thu | 10–15 personalised first messages/day; reply to all responses same day |
| Wed | 1 LinkedIn post (founder-led) |
| Fri | Follow-ups (day 3 / day 7 / day 14 sequence), update pipeline, review numbers |

Pipeline stages: Prospect → Contacted → Replied → Call booked → Proposal → Won/Lost (record reason).

## 3. Pitches (core angles)
- **Website:** "Your site isn't turning visitors into enquiries — here are 3 specific fixes." (Point to genuine, observed issues.)
- **Mobile app:** "Your customers already use their phones for X; an app could let them Y."
- **Custom software:** "You're running [process] across [tools] — one system could remove the re-typing."
- **AI integration:** "Start with one measurable workflow; we'll prototype it before you commit."
- **International outsourcing:** "An India-based team with overlapping hours, written scopes and code you own."
- **Agency partnership:** "Sell more web/app projects without hiring — we build under your brand."
Proof to attach: BrokrSuite (live on both stores), DMGennie, QuantMentor, relevant service page, a relevant Insights article.

## 4. BrokrSuite acquisition
Covered in depth in `BROKRSUITE_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md`. Targets: brokerage companies, property dealers, agencies, consultants, sales teams, channel partners. Channel mix: local association events and workshops, LinkedIn for agency owners, Instagram/Facebook Reels showing the app, WhatsApp follow-up only after a person has engaged (enquiry, event, or explicit permission).

## 5. Free and low-cost inbound
- Google Business Profile (if eligible) with services, photos and posts.
- Directory profiles (see `OFF_PAGE_SEO.md`) — ask every happy client for an honest review.
- Insights articles shared on LinkedIn with UTM links.
- Founder posts: lessons from building BrokrSuite and DMGennie.

## 6. 30 / 60 / 90-day plan

| Window | Development services | BrokrSuite |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 1–30** | Finish manual setup (Search Console, GTM events, Clarity). Build prospect list of 100. Send 150 personalised messages. Create LinkedIn company page. Publish 2 more articles. | Contact 50 brokers/agencies personally; run 1 local workshop or webinar; book 10 demos; collect feedback. |
| **Days 31–60** | Clutch/GoodFirms/DesignRush profiles; first 2 client reviews; 1 guest article; refine pitches by reply rate. | Convert first paying customers; first customer story (with permission); referral ask; 2 association partnerships. |
| **Days 61–90** | Double down on the 2 best-performing segments; publish first client case study; consider small paid LinkedIn test with UTM tracking. | Product Hunt launch (if positioned for global audience) or Indian SaaS directories; publish comparison/criteria content on brokrsuite.in; expand to a second city. |

## 7. Measure
Messages sent → reply rate → calls → proposals → wins, per segment and channel. Website leads by source (enquiry email "Lead source" block + GA4). Customer acquisition cost once spend exists (time + tools + ads ÷ customers won).
